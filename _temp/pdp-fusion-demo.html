Product Overview

One Platform. Endless Possibilities.

The PT500 is a modular VTOL UAV platform designed to adapt to your mission. Hot-swap payloads in seconds, fly in rain or shine, and capture data with centimeter-level precision.

Precision Engineering from the Ground Up

Every component of the PT500 is designed for reliability in mission-critical operations. The carbon fiber airframe reduces weight while maintaining structural integrity in winds up to 15 m/s.

  • Foldable arms for rapid deployment — airborne in under 60 seconds
  • Dual RTK + PPK positioning — 1 cm accuracy without ground control points
  • IP55-rated ingress protection — operate in rain, dust, and extreme temperatures
  • Triple-redundant IMU & compass — fail-safe navigation in GPS-denied environments

Key Features

Designed for Professionals

Every feature of the PT500 is purpose-built to solve real operational challenges.

Swap Payloads in Seconds

The quick-release gimbal system lets you hot-swap between RGB, thermal, LiDAR, and multispectral payloads without tools or rebooting.

  • Tool-free quick-release mechanism with auto-locking latch
  • Automatic payload recognition — no manual configuration needed
  • Compatible with third-party sensors via open SDK

Centimeter-Level Precision

Dual RTK + PPK positioning delivers survey-grade accuracy without ground control points. Ideal for topographic mapping and cadastral surveys.

  • 1 cm + 1 ppm horizontal accuracy with RTK fix
  • PPK backup ensures accuracy even when RTK signal is lost
  • Seamless integration with major GIS and CAD software

Any Weather, Any Time

IP55-rated protection and 15 m/s wind resistance mean the PT500 flies when others can't. Rain, dust, snow, or extreme temperatures — the mission continues.

  • Sealed motor housings and corrosion-resistant fasteners
  • Self-heating intelligent battery for sub-zero operation
  • Millimeter-wave radar for all-weather obstacle avoidance
